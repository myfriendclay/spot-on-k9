Drop-in group classes

Held in Central Park West at 59th Street, in the Columbus Circle area.

You'll practise obedience commands, learn new handling and defensive street skills, play engagement games, take on agility challenges, meet other dog owners, get your questions answered — and have a genuinely good time doing it.

Everyone in the class has already finished (or nearly finished) private training, so you're among peers working through the same problems. That's what keeps people consistent — at no extra charge.

Schedule

Classes usually run on the last Saturday of the month, March through November, starting at 9:30 am. If bad weather is forecast we move to the following Saturday or a nearby date; any cancellation is posted on the site by 8:00 am.

Before you come

  • RSVP by email at least 24 hours before the class
  • Cancel an RSVP 24 hours ahead so it isn't marked as used
  • Classes are one hour and start on time
  • Dogs must be on a leash
  • Bring a treat bag, placemat, your dog's favourite treats, kibble and water
  • Two people maximum per dog
  • Up to 20 people and 18 dogs per class

Dates, times and the number of classes can change without notice. The drop-in programme is an added-value offering and can be modified or withdrawn at any time.
