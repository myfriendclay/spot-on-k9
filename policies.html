Cancellations & missed appointments

You may change your reservation for any service up to 36 hours before the scheduled time. Cancel or miss an appointment with less than 36 hours' notice and you'll be charged the full session fee.

Unconfirmed classes are forfeited

To be fair to other clients trying to book time: when we send you specific dates for your class schedule, you have five days to confirm or cancel them. If we don't hear from you in that window, those dates are cancelled and the classes are forfeited — without refund, exchange or change of date.

Class expiration

Paid-for services must be used within four months of the purchase date.

Refunds & exchanges

We have a strict no-refund policy.

  • No refunds, exchanges or transfers for scheduled services.
  • Classes can only be rescheduled if cancelled with at least 36 hours' notice.
  • Clients agree not to request refunds, exchanges or transfers for unused or missed classes or services — including if you change your mind about training after it has started.
Payments

All training and services are paid in full online by credit card before the scheduled sessions. Visa, MasterCard and PayPal are accepted. Payment is what holds your dates.

Ask about cash pricing — it's available on most packages.

Follow-up support

After any training lesson, if questions or problems come up, email or call during business hours, Monday through Sunday. With few exceptions, calls and emails are returned the same day or within 24 hours.

That commitment doesn't expire with your package — it's for your dog's life.

Drop-in group classes

After completing a training package you can transition into the free drop-in group classes. They're held March through November in Central Park West — not during snow or rain, or when temperatures fall below 34°F or climb above 84°F. You'll be given the exact time and location at the end of your classes.

Please note:

  • RSVP by email at least 24 hours before the class you want to attend.
  • Classes are 60 minutes and start on time.
  • Dogs must be on a leash.
  • Maximum of two dog owners per dog.
  • Limit of 20 people (NYC Central Park rules) and 18 dogs per class.

The drop-in programme is an added-value offering. Dates, times, the number of monthly classes and the length of the offering are subject to change or removal without notice. Spot-On K9 Training can cancel individual classes or stop offering drop-in group classes at any time. The number of free drop-in classes is also subject to change.

Photos & videos

Photos and videos of your dog may be taken while working with Spot-On K9 Training, for use in marketing or training materials. By using Spot-On K9 Training you agree to allow us to use your dog's name and any images or likenesses taken during our walking or training programs.

We will never disclose your name without your prior written consent.

Referral program

The referral program is how we say “I Ruff You.” For each newly referred client, we'll Venmo you $40 — or send a gift certificate for the same. Very Spot-On.

Travel fees & pricing
  • Connecticut: a travel fee applies for locations more than 40 minutes from Monroe, CT.
  • Other boroughs or areas we don't cover: please ask about availability and possible travel fees.
  • In some areas, depending on travel time and transportation, in-person training isn't available.
  • Prices are subject to change.
  • Package prices don't include suggested training tools.
Eligibility & weather

We don't train dogs under 14 weeks old, or (in Connecticut) under 10 lbs.

Classes are rescheduled if it rains or snows, or if temperatures fall below 34°F or rise above 84°F — high winds are taken into account. Any group-class cancellation is posted on the site by 8:00 am.
