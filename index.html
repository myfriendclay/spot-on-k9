What we work on

If your dog is doing any of this, you're in the right place

Most dogs that need help aren't badly behaved — they're missing structure, leadership and a way to understand what you're asking. That's what we build.

  • Leash reactivity — barking, lunging, screaming
  • Dog-on-dog & people aggression
  • Resource guarding food, toys or people
  • Pulling and lunging on the leash

The method

Positively Balanced Training®

Training is more than a list of commands and a bag of treats. What's missing in almost every dog that needs behavioural help is the same short list: rules, structure, leadership, accountability and clear communication.

We start at the root cause of the behaviour rather than papering over the symptom, and we teach you to hold the standard — because you're the one who lives with the dog. Most owners see a meaningful change inside the first session.

Tools we may use: slip leashes, prong collars, remote e-collars, food and toys, play, and controlled exposure to people and other dogs for distraction and socialisation. Having a range of options means we can pick the gentlest one that actually works for your dog.
