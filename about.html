What's usually missing

Training is more than a list of commands or a bag of tools. It's about understanding your dog. And in almost every dog that needs behavioural help, the same six things are missing:

  • Rules
  • Structure
  • Leadership
  • Accountability
  • Obedience
  • Clear communication

We build those first. Everything else follows from them — and we start at the root cause of the behaviour rather than managing the symptom.

Tools I use — and why

I use a range of tools and methods, because having options means I can reach for the gentlest thing that actually works for the dog in front of me rather than forcing one approach onto every dog.

  • Slip leashes and prong collars
  • Remote e-collars, introduced at low levels
  • Food, toys and play
  • People and other dogs, for controlled distraction and socialisation

I'll always explain what I'm using, why, and how to use it properly — you're the one who has to live with the dog after I go home.
