Serious cases

Serious Aggression Rehab (SARC)

For owners genuinely struggling with dangerous behaviour — a bite history, serious aggression toward people or dogs. SARC isn't billed per hour or per lesson; it's a case, priced per case (most run about $1,600) and it runs until the case is handled. Includes 12 free drop-in group classes and 12 months of phone and email support.
